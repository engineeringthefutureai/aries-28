# Part: Power bay

## Purpose & role
Mounting and organization for the power supply, fuse/distribution block, and dressed wiring on the facilities deck. Positioned low for stability and, in the styled build, displayed through a smoked side panel. Requires a PSU air gap and venting (see design doc thermal notes).

## Fabrication route(s)
`[PRINT]` mounts/combs + `[BUY]` (PSU, fuse block, connectors).

See the fabrication legend in [`../README.md`](../README.md) for method tags and material notes (PETG vs. PLA, acrylic, buy-vs-fabricate).

## Status
Pending. Layout follows the chosen PSU and fuse-block dimensions.

## Dependencies
- Frame — the bay occupies the lower deck footprint.
- Wiring/fusing — see design doc §4.
