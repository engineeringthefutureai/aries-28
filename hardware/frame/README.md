# Part: Frame

## Purpose & role
The structural skeleton: black anodized 2020 aluminum extrusion in a compact tower form. Corner hardware is bought (metal, for squareness); everything mounted to the frame is fabricated or bought separately.

## Fabrication route(s)
`[BUY]` — extrusion cut to length, metal corner brackets/connectors, M5 T-nuts and screws. Frame squareness depends on accurate cuts (miter saw with a non-ferrous blade) plus metal corners.

See the fabrication legend in [`../README.md`](../README.md) for method tags and material notes (PETG vs. PLA, acrylic, buy-vs-fabricate).

## Status
Specified; cut plan (segment lengths) pending finalized internal dimensions.

## Dependencies
- All mounted parts reference the frame as datum.
- Panels, racks, and decks are sized to the assembled frame.
